[ ] DNS: apex domain + email authentication  (needs Namecheap access)
    Deferred 2026-08-04. Requires DNS control at Namecheap
    (dns1/dns2.registrar-servers.com), which is outside GitHub/Azure.

    1. Apex domain srilatha.art does not resolve.
       TWO independent causes - fixing either alone does nothing:

       a) DNS points to the wrong place:
              srilatha.art      A     162.255.119.151   <- Namecheap parking
              www.srilatha.art  CNAME salmon-wave-01c7b8300.7.azurestaticapps.net  (correct)

       b) Azure does not know about the apex. Custom domains on
          swa-thesrilathaarts-prd contain ONLY www.srilatha.art.

       Fix (recommended - real HTTPS on the bare domain):
         - Register srilatha.art on the SWA with dns-txt-token validation
         - Add the returned TXT record at _dnsauth.srilatha.art  (Namecheap)
         - Replace the apex A record with an ALIAS record pointing to
           salmon-wave-01c7b8300.7.azurestaticapps.net           (Namecheap)

       Do NOT use an A record to the SWA IP. It currently resolves to
       172.169.198.52 via Traffic Manager, but Azure rotates those - it
       would break silently later.

       Cheaper alternative: Namecheap URL Redirect srilatha.art -> https://www.srilatha.art
       Works, but https://srilatha.art shows a certificate warning first,
       because Namecheap cannot serve a cert for your domain.

    2. No DMARC record.  <-- highest impact of the three
       _dmarc.srilatha.art does not exist. SPF and Zoho MX are present.

       Why it matters here: this domain sends order confirmations and
       invoices. Without DMARC anyone can forge mail as @srilatha.art and
       receivers have no policy to apply - a phishing vector against your
       customers using your brand. Gmail and Yahoo also now require DMARC
       for bulk senders, so transactional mail is likelier to land in spam.

       Start in monitor mode (changes nothing about delivery):
           _dmarc.srilatha.art  TXT  "v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:studio@srilatha.art"
       Tighten to p=quarantine once the reports look clean.

    3. SPF is soft-fail.
       Current: v=spf1 include:zohomail.in ~all
       "~all" tells receivers "probably not us, but accept anyway".
       Move to "-all" after DMARC monitoring confirms nothing legitimate fails.

    4. No CAA record. Low severity, one record:
           srilatha.art  CAA  0 issue "digicert.com"
       (Azure SWA certificates are issued by DigiCert.)
