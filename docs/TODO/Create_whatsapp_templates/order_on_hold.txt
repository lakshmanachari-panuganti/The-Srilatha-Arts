7. order_on_hold
Purpose

Used when the order cannot proceed until the customer provides something.

Examples:

Missing reference photos
Incomplete address
Phone number not reachable
Custom artwork clarification needed
Payment verification issue
Courier service unavailable for the destination
Trigger
Order Status:
→ ON_HOLD
Variables
Variable	Meaning
{{1}}	Customer Name
{{2}}	Order Number
{{3}}	Hold Reason
Example Template
Hello {{1}},

Your order {{2}} is currently on hold.

Reason:
{{3}}

Please reply to this message or contact us so we can continue working on your order.

Thank you,
Srilatha Art
Sample Message
Hello Lakshman,

Your order SA-20260611-001 is currently on hold.

Reason:
We need one additional reference photograph for your custom artwork.

Please reply to this message or contact us so we can continue working on your order.

Thank you,
Srilatha Art
Business Value

Without this template:

Customer waits
Customer gets worried
Customer contacts support

With this template:

Customer immediately knows why work stopped
